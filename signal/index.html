Dana Okonkwo is a senior solutions engineer working in cloud infrastructure. She helps customer-facing technical teams turn ambiguous constraints into decisions they can act on, and has spent the last decade in platform and pre-sales roles at mid-size SaaS companies.

How I help

  1. Discovery

    Finding the constraint a customer actually has, which is usually not the one in the requirements document.

  2. Tradeoff analysis

    Laying out what each option costs later, in operational load and in the freedom it removes from the next team.

  3. Handoff design

    Building the foundation so the next engineer can extend it rather than route around it.
